Product Requirements Document (PRD)
AI Product Intelligence Operating System
Version: 1.0Status: DraftAuthor: Product TeamDate: June 2026
Executive Summary
The AI Product Intelligence Operating System is a unified platform designed to help product, growth, marketing, and business teams make better decisions through data-driven insights, automated hypothesis generation, and machine-learning-powered validation.
The platform combines performance analytics, historical experiment intelligence, conversational AI, and automated statistical analysis into a single workspace. Instead of requiring users to manually analyze reports, formulate assumptions, and coordinate with data teams, the platform enables a seamless workflow from insight discovery to validated business recommendations.
The core objective is to reduce the time required to identify growth opportunities, understand performance changes, and make informed product decisions.

Problem Statement
Modern organizations rely on multiple disconnected tools for analytics, reporting, experimentation, and business intelligence.
Teams often face challenges such as:
Fragmented access to performance data.
Lack of centralized experiment history.
Manual and time-consuming data analysis.
Difficulty identifying root causes behind metric changes.
Inconsistent hypothesis generation.
Dependence on analysts and data scientists for validation.
As a result, decision-making is slower, insights are missed, and organizational knowledge becomes scattered across documents, dashboards, and reports.
There is a growing need for an intelligent platform that can centralize data, surface insights automatically, generate hypotheses, and validate those hypotheses using advanced machine learning techniques.

Product Vision
To build an AI-powered decision intelligence platform that transforms raw business data into validated growth opportunities and actionable recommendations.
The platform will serve as a central operating system for product and business teams, enabling them to move from observation to action with minimal manual effort.
