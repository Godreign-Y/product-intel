# PRODUCT ARCHETYPES

## Purpose

Defines reusable business behavior templates.

Every product belongs to exactly one archetype.

Archetypes determine:

- Price Elasticity
- Discount Elasticity
- Marketing Sensitivity
- Retention Dynamics
- Inventory Sensitivity
- Channel Preference
- Acquisition Preference
- Seasonality Strength

---

# POC Scope

Current POC supports 10 archetypes.

Future versions may add unlimited archetypes without changing simulator architecture.
