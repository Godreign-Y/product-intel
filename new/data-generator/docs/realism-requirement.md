# REALISM REQUIREMENTS

## Action Effects

Every action must have:

- Activation
- Peak
- Decay

No instantaneous permanent effects allowed.

---

## Multiple Actions

Simulator must support:

- Single Action
- Sequential Actions
- Overlapping Actions

---

## Interaction Effects

Actions may:

- Amplify
- Suppress
- Saturate
- Cannibalize

other actions.

---

## Noise

Every KPI must include stochastic variation.

---

## Seasonality

Support:

- Weekly
- Monthly
- Quarterly
- Annual

patterns.

---

## Market Shocks

Support:

- Demand Spike
- Demand Drop
- Inventory Shock
- Competitor Shock

events.

---

## Product Heterogeneity

Products belonging to different archetypes must react differently to identical actions.

Example:

Price +10%

Luxury Product

Traffic Change:
-2%

Commodity FMCG

Traffic Change:
-12%
